An entry for the [make.fun bounty "Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-riidzhsn-angry-judge-the-short-rant-contest-b9f46046): one short, funny rant about a small everyday annoyance, at most 400 characters.

## The entry

> I scanned one bag of rice and the self-checkout screamed UNEXPECTED ITEM IN THE BAGGING AREA. Twelve people watched a robot accuse me of smuggling rice in my socks while the attendant strolled over, pressed one button, and left like a hero.

240 characters, two sentences. The anger is shared, not performed: a machine publicly accuses an innocent shopper of smuggling rice in his socks, in front of a queue of twelve people, and the human fix is one button pressed by an attendant who strolls off like a hero.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the rant to `/output/entry.txt`. Set `OUTPUT_PATH` to write it somewhere else for a local run.
